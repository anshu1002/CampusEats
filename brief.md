# CampusEats Brief

## What

CampusEats is a campus food ordering system. It helps students find food available on campus and order it without having to visit different food providers manually. The system also helps food providers manage their food items and incoming orders.

## Who

The main users of CampusEats are:

- **Students:** They can view food items, place orders, and check the status of their orders.
- **Food Providers:** They can add or manage food items and handle student orders.
- **Admin:** The admin manages users, food providers, and the overall system.

- Place Order
- Cancel Order
- Make Payment
- Track Order

These verbs represent the main actions or tasks performed by users and the system.

## Summary

CampusEats connects students with campus food providers through a simple ordering system. Students can discover food and place orders, food providers can manage their menus and orders, and the admin can manage the overall platform.
