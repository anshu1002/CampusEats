# CampusEats

CampusEats is a food ordering system for students on campus. It makes it easier for students to see what food is available and place an order.

## What does it do?

Students can check the available food items, choose what they want, place an order and check their order status. Food providers can add food items and manage the orders they receive.

## Who uses it?

- **Students:** They can view food items, place orders and check their order status.
- **Food Providers:** They can add food items and manage student orders.
- **Admin:** The admin manages users, food providers and the overall system.

## Main Features

- View available food items
- Place an order
- Check order status
- Add and manage food items
- Manage users and orders
